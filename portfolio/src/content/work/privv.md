## What it is

Privv is a dedicated operating system for building owners and owner's representatives managing large-scale capital projects. It consolidates budgeting, scheduling, procurement, and invoicing into a single platform driven by AI insights.

My focus was on the frontend and product-system build. The core objective was establishing a maintainable, scalable foundation that could handle complex, data-heavy views—like real-time cash flow visibility and predictive budget insights—without degrading performance.

## Why it matters

Platforms handling multi-million-dollar capital budgets cannot afford brittle UI states or sluggish data grids. Frontend codebases rapidly degrade when delivery outpaces structural improvement, especially in enterprise software with sprawling feature requirements. This work prioritized the constraints that dictate long-term velocity and reliability: strict state shape, predictable data flow, and interface consistency.

## Technical shape

The technical output is a frontend system structured to allow continuous product development without accumulating crippling technical debt. By treating architecture as a first-class product requirement, the platform can safely scale its AI-driven financial foresight and scheduling tools across university, healthcare, and municipal construction projects.
