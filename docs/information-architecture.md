# Information Architecture

## MVP Pages

### Home

Purpose:
- establish positioning fast
- show current focus and selected proof
- route serious visitors to the right next step

Include:
- headline and short positioning
- current focus summary
- selected work preview
- workflow summary
- contact CTA

Exclude:
- life story
- skill badges
- generic feature-card filler

### Now

Purpose:
- show current work and active direction

Include:
- current project focus
- what is being learned or refined
- immediate priorities
- what is intentionally not being done

Exclude:
- stale logs
- vague aspirations

### Work

Purpose:
- show proof through a small number of credible project summaries

Include:
- 1-3 strong projects
- role, scope, constraints, and decisions

Exclude:
- toy projects
- everything ever built

### Workflow

Purpose:
- explain how engineering judgment and AI-assisted leverage fit together

Include:
- planning approach
- implementation stance
- review and ownership model
- maintainability and delivery standards

Exclude:
- AI hype
- tool worship

### Contact

Purpose:
- convert real interest into a useful conversation

Include:
- email
- LinkedIn
- fit statement for relevant work

Exclude:
- noisy forms
- vague networking language
